# Merveil AI — Investor / Client Demo Script

## Opening — 30 seconds

“Merveil AI is the intelligence layer beyond interaction. What you are about to see is the flagship experience, but the bigger product is the infrastructure behind it. The same identity, trust, connection, AI and voice capabilities can be consumed by companies through Merveil APIs.”

## Act 1 — Trust

Open Passport.

Show the trusted identity concept and explain that verification/context is the foundation for safer interactions.

**Message:** “We start with trust before intelligence.”

## Act 2 — Connection

Open Connect.

Show the citizen/network experience, online/offline state and messaging.

**Message:** “Merveil is designed around meaningful, intelligent connection rather than simple follower mechanics.”

## Act 3 — Intelligence

Open Merveil AI Assist.

Demonstrate a useful task rather than a generic chatbot question.

**Message:** “The AI understands the Merveil context and becomes useful inside the interaction layer.”

## Act 4 — Voice

Open Merveil AI Call separately from AI Assist.

Show the call concept and, when device certification is complete, demonstrate microphone, language and voice selection.

**Message:** “Voice is its own infrastructure layer. AI Assist and Merveil AI Call are distinct products.”

## Act 5 — World

Open World Reels and Creator Studio.

Show creator publishing and discovery.

Then show Pulse Reels and clarify:

**Message:** “World is global. Pulse is specifically built for real estate.”

## Act 6 — Commercial ecosystem

Show property/investor workflows and marketplace surfaces.

**Message:** “The same trusted layer can power professional and commercial interactions.”

## Act 7 — Developer Platform — the key investor moment

Open the Developer Platform.

Show the three paths:

- Build with Merveil
- Ask Merveil
- Grow with Merveil

Then open API Playground.

Run a safe test request and show:

- response
- latency
- request ID
- credit usage
- scope/authentication model

Open Webhooks / Usage / Applications.

**Message:** “This is where Merveil stops being only an application. Companies can build on the intelligence layer.”

## Act 8 — Business model

Show the commercial plans:

- Sandbox — $0
- Developer — $9
- Growth — $49
- Business — $199
- Enterprise — custom

**Message:** “The entry point is intentionally accessible. As usage and business value grow, Merveil grows with the client.”

## Closing

“Merveil is not trying to win with one feature. We are building a connected intelligence layer where trusted identity, connection, AI, voice, world content and developer infrastructure reinforce each other. The app proves the system. The APIs make the system scalable.”

## Demo discipline

- Never claim live payment capture unless it has passed live end-to-end verification.
- Never claim WebRTC or AI Call is certified from architecture alone.
- Never use mock data as if it were a live customer result.
- If a capability is not device-certified, say so clearly.
- Keep the API Playground demonstration short and concrete.
- Finish on the infrastructure/API story, not on a feature carousel.
