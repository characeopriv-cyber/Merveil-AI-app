How to Play

  • Drag from one node to another to connect.
  • Gold = investor (bonus). Red = risk (penalty).
  • Chain links for combo multipliers.
  • Regions unlock as score rises — Gulf → Europe → Asia → Americas → Africa.
  • Beat the timer. Higher levels are shorter and denser.
