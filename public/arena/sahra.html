How to Play

  • Tap left / right or use arrow keys to steer while falling.
  • Land on safe dune ledges — avoid dark hazard platforms.
  • Collect water drops to charge DASH for speed and brief protection.
  • Regions: Dunes → Wadi → Oasis → Hajar — speed rises as you go deeper.
  • You have 3 lives.
