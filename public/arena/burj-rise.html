How to Play

  • Tap or Space to drop the moving 3D brick.
  • Align with the block below for a Perfect Stack bonus.
  • Misaligned drops trim the brick and lower Integrity.
  • Reach the floor goal to complete the stop and unlock the next tower.
  • Speed rises with height and later stops.
