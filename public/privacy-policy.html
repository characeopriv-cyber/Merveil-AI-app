Privacy Policy

Merveil AI · Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Contact: privacy@junction.technology

Merveil AI (“we”, “us”) is a super-app for verified identity (Passport), networking, careers, services, investment discovery, World reels, and trusted connections. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. Data we collect

  • Account & identity: email, phone (if provided), display name, profile photo, Passport fields you submit for verification, verification status.
  • Content you create: World posts and reels, comments, messages, marketplace or invest posts, reports.
  • Usage: screens viewed, feature interactions, crash diagnostics, approximate device type and app/browser version.
  • Device & push: push subscription endpoints or FCM tokens so we can send notifications you enable; optional camera/microphone only when you record or call.
  • Network: IP address and standard server logs for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use data

  • Provide the service (login, Passport, messaging, World, marketplace, Arena credits tied to your account).
  • Verify identity and reduce fraud.
  • Send notifications you request (messages, calls, product updates).
  • Moderate illegal or abusive content and enforce Terms.
  • Improve reliability and performance.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

  • Infrastructure: hosting, database, and storage providers that process data under contract (e.g. cloud hosting, Supabase).
  • Legal: when required by law or to protect users and the platform.
  • Other users: content you choose to publish (profile, World, marketplace) is visible according to product settings.

4. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active. You may request deletion. Backups and legal holds may retain limited data for a short period. Public posts you delete are removed from active feeds subject to technical delay.

5. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and row-level security on application data. No method is 100% secure; protect your login and device.

6. Your rights

Depending on your region you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data. Contact privacy@junction.technology. To delete your account from the app: open Passport / Settings → request account deletion, or email the same address from the email on your account.

7. Children

Merveil AI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

8. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries where our providers operate. We use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
